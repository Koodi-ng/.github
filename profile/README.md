# Koodi

### Financial access starts with a conversation.

We are building voice banking for everyday Nigerians, making financial services accessible through a simple phone call.

Our focus is straightforward: an experience that works on ordinary phones, communicates clearly, and puts security and reliability first.

### What we are building

- **Koodi Account** — access to a Koodi account through a voice-led experience.
- **Koodi Link** — a way to connect supported existing bank accounts to the Koodi experience.
- **The platform** — the voice, payments and security infrastructure behind those experiences.

Product availability depends on rollout and supported services. Visit our website for current information.
